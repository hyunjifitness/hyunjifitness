01 — About

Hi, I'm Hyunji.

I started coaching in college. I was competing in taekwondo when the chance came up to teach the cardio kickboxing PE class, and I ended up teaching it for four years.

After that I spent several years as a mechanical engineer, which changed how I think about the body: how forces travel through joints, why small alignment details matter, and how to build strength that holds up over time.

I missed working with people, so I earned my ACSM Certified Personal Trainer credential and started coaching again — first over Zoom, then in person. Today I train clients one-on-one at Expansive Strength & Fitness on Cedar Street.

Outside the gym you'll find me playing volleyball, baking sourdough, and walking my poodle.

Credentials

  • ACSM Certified Personal Trainer
  • B.S. & M.Eng., Mechanical Engineering — Cornell University
  • CPR/AED certified
  • Four years teaching university group fitness
  • Competitive taekwondo and volleyball background
02 — Approach

How I coach

Four things shape every session I run.

01

Your body isn't a project to fix

No talk about earning food or burning off the weekend. We focus on what your body can do and what you want it to do next.

02

Mechanics before load

My engineering background shows up in the details: how you move, where you compensate, and building a base that keeps you training for years.

03

Progress you can feel

Groceries up three flights without thinking. Back to your sport. Deadlifting your bodyweight. Goals that matter to you, tracked session to session.

04

Training you look forward to

If you dread your workouts, they won't last. The best program is the one you're still doing a year from now.

Who
