Personal Training — Somerville, MA

Fitness for everybody.

I'm a personal trainer who cares about how your body feels, not how it looks. What we track is what your body can do, and how it feels to do it. One of my favorite parts of this work is helping people unlock strength they didn't know they had.

My focus is health you can carry for decades. Strength that holds up, joints that move easily, less pain in the places that have bothered you for years, and a clearer sense of connection to your own body. Training pays off over a lifetime through your strength, your metabolic health, your mood, your ability to handle a stressful week.

I also believe exercise should be something you enjoy rather than something you endure. Movement becomes a real part of your life when it feels good and you look forward to it, so we'll find the version of training you actually like and let consistency do the work.

I don't have one program that everyone follows. Your history, your schedule, your injuries, the sport you play, and what you want from this are all your own. We'll build around the body and the life you have, and keep adjusting as both of them change.

About me

I'm Hyunji, an ACSM Certified Personal Trainer passionate about bringing fitness to everybody.

I joined the taekwondo team in college as a white belt with no martial arts background, and it taught me two things I still coach from: that getting better is mostly about showing up, and that moving your body feels good, especially when you're doing it alongside other people. That led me to teaching cardio kickboxing PE classes in college, where I found out I loved coaching.

These days most of my own training revolves around volleyball. A lot of what I do in the gym is built around it — jumping higher, landing better, keeping my shoulders happy. It keeps me honest about what it's like to have a goal you care about and a body with its own opinions about how to get there.

I work with people who are brand new to exercising, people coming back after years away, and athletes chasing something specific. Wherever you're starting, we build from there.
